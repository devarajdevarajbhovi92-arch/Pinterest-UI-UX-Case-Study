# Pinterest – Product Teardown & UI/UX Case Study

## Project Overview
A product teardown of Pinterest analyzing its user experience, business model, strengths, and user pain points. The case study proposes two improvements: AI Content Transparency & Filter and Project Mode to help users move from inspiration to real-world execution.

## Key Problems Identified
- Difficulty distinguishing AI-generated Pins from authentic content.
- Lack of tools to convert saved inspiration into actionable projects.

## Proposed Improvements

### 1. AI Content Transparency & Filter
- AI-generated content labels.
- Content filters for All, Human-Created, and AI-Generated Pins.
- Improved content transparency and user trust.

### 2. Project Mode
- Convert saved Pins into actionable projects.
- Project creation and configuration.
- Checklists, timelines, and milestones.
- Budget and progress tracking.
- Project completion and success tracking.

## Product Analysis
- Product overview and target users.
- Product strengths and business model.
- User pain-point identification.
- Proposed solutions and expected impact.
- KPIs for engagement, retention, discovery, and project completion.

## Design Process
- Product teardown and analysis.
- User problem identification.
- Feature ideation and prioritization.
- User journey and flow design.
- Wireframing and interactive prototyping.
